How it works

Three steps from idle PC to income

The contract handles listing, escrow, health verification and settlement. There is no platform in between.

01

List Your Machine

Publish CPU, RAM, storage, OS and an hourly price on-chain, then point the contract at your machine's /health endpoint.

02

Someone Rents It

A renter picks their hours and deposits into escrow. You accept or decline in-app — chat is on-chain, so the negotiation is part of the record.

03

Get Paid for Uptime

Every hour your machine answers its health check, that hour's payment is released to you. Hours it was down are refunded to the renter.

Why NodePool

Akash and Gensyn need massive infrastructure to run their marketplace. NodePool's smart contract is the infrastructure.

Pricing

Traditional VPS bills the calendar.
NodePool bills the uptime.

Same machine, same hour — the difference is what happens when it goes down.

Traditional VPS NodePool
Billing model ✕Flat monthly fee, paid upfront ✓Per hour, only when verified online
When it goes down ✕You pay anyway — credits at their discretion ✓Payment pauses that hour, automatically
Refunds ✕Support ticket, days of waiting ✓Unused deposit returns on rental end
Uptime proof ✕Trust the vendor's status page ✓Every health check recorded on-chain
Commitment ✕Monthly or annual lock-in ✓End any time, settle instantly
Who takes a cut ✕The cloud provider's margin ✓Nobody — provider and renter, direct
Example: a 0.005 ETH/hr machine rented for 24 hours that drops offline for 3 hours settles at 0.105 ETH — the 3 offline hours are refunded, not invoiced.
